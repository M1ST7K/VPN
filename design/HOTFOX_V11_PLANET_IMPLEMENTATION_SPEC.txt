HOTFOX V11 — PLANET IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION

SOURCE ASSETS
Use these V11 assets as visual truth:
- design/assets/v11/planet/hotfox_planet_reference_a.webp
- design/assets/v11/planet/hotfox_planet_reference_b.webp
- bootstrap/hotfox_2_2_0/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/hf_fox_bust_transparent.png (existing clean production fox foreground)
- bootstrap/hotfox_2_2_0/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/hf_fox_planet.png (existing historical combined reference; do not restore as production composite)

The first two are compact isolated V11 planet references for composition/color/texture guidance. The exact full approved screen boards in `design/hotfox_18_final_style_reference/` remain the final visual authority. The existing clean transparent fox resource in `drawable-nodpi/hf_fox_bust_transparent.png` is the production foreground source. The older combined `hf_fox_planet.png` may be inspected only as a historical composition reference and must not be restored as a baked production composite.

GOAL
Implement a reusable responsive planet backdrop that preserves the approved appearance as closely as technically possible while allowing per-screen crop/scale/offset. Do not recreate the planet as a simplified VectorDrawable arc.

LAYER MODEL
1. page background;
2. planet raster/backdrop layer derived from approved V11 references;
3. optional subtle atmospheric overlay if required by reference;
4. clean transparent fox foreground;
5. native UI text and controls.

Never bake text/buttons/status UI into the planet or fox bitmap.

FOX ALPHA SAFETY
- Inspect alpha bounds and semi-transparent edge pixels of the production fox foreground.
- Planet must sit entirely behind fox.
- No bright rim may leak through fox interior.
- No bright fragments may poke through neck/ear/fur edges because of wrong scale/offset.
- Test against black and the actual HotFox page background.
- If needed, reposition planet rim so fox physically occludes the strongest portion.
- Do not erode fox alpha or hide mistakes with dark matte rectangles.

FIDELITY RULES
The planet has a large dark spherical mass, mostly black interior, restrained surface texture, warm copper/orange directional rim concentrated on the upper/right edge, weak illumination on the darker side, and smooth atmospheric transition to black.

Do not use:
- uniform orange outline;
- two parallel hard arcs;
- flat gradient circle;
- stars/nebulae/orbit dots/radar rings;
- bright sci-fi glow;
- generic Material/vector semicircle.

RESPONSIVE HERO SYSTEM
Create explicit composition parameters for at least:
- compact phone;
- regular phone;
- small tablet portrait;
- large tablet portrait.

Control independently per breakpoint:
- hero container width/height;
- fox scale and X/Y offset;
- planet scale and X/Y offset;
- crop mode;
- max width;
- distance to title/subtitle;
- distance to CTA/cards/navigation.

Preserve aspect ratio. Never non-uniformly stretch the planet.

PHONE BEHAVIOR
Use a tighter crop, keep fox dominant, show enough sphere to read as a planet, and prevent CTA/nav collisions.

TABLET BEHAVIOR
Do not leave a tiny centered hero in a huge void. Increase max hero size intentionally, preserve fox/planet relationship, constrain text reading width, and adapt CTA width/position instead of simply stretching phone UI.

PER-SCREEN CONTRACT
01 Splash: strongest cinematic crop; fox+planet centered as a brand scene; planet supports, not dominates.
02 Connect HotFox: same planet family with slightly quieter crop; preserve text and CTA hierarchy.
05 Disconnected: hero fits between heading and permission/CTA blocks; planet rim never touches CTA.
06 Connecting: same planet family; progress stage graphic remains separate.
07 Protected: same planet family; can be slightly calmer/darker for stats, but planet is not a success indicator.
08 Sheet: use live 05 composition behind the sheet, not a duplicate implementation.
09 HTTPS subscription: only if fox hero remains; compact, lower-contrast supporting crop.

VISUAL QA
For every planet screen capture at minimum:
- 1080x2400 regular phone portrait;
- one compact phone profile;
- one small tablet portrait;
- one large tablet portrait.

Check for alpha leakage, hard crop seams, stretch, visual identity drift, text/CTA collision, giant dead space and excessive hero shrinkage. Preserve V10 evidence and write V11 evidence to new folders.
