3. PLANET REBUILD — MANDATORY
====================================
This is mandatory across screens where the planet is present.

Target screens: 01, 02, 05, 07, 08 and 09.
Potentially also any other screen where the hero composition benefits from a subtle planet anchor.

The planet must follow these rules:
1. It must read as a large, dark, atmospheric planetary mass — not a decorative orange arc.
2. The fill should be extremely dark, close to the background, but still distinguishable through soft volume.
3. Use a soft copper/orange rim light only where it makes physical/compositional sense.
4. Rim light must not be uniform all the way around. It should feel cinematic and directional.
5. The planet should not visually fight with the fox. The fox remains the hero.
6. The planet must create depth and framing, not visual noise.
7. Crop and scale must be consistent screen-to-screen so the brand system feels intentional.
8. Avoid harsh outline feel. Avoid “half-circle sticker” look. Avoid obvious generic gradient sphere.
9. The fox should feel embedded into a premium space composition, not pasted over a circle.
10. Planet treatment must be subtle enough that UI remains primary.

Planet visual guidance:
- dark sphere body
- low-contrast inner volume
- elegant atmospheric edge
- slightly different crop per screen allowed, but one system
- believable soft fade into background

If needed, create a reusable hero background component with screen-specific parameters.
